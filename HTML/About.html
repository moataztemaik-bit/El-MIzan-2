Project Story

Managing money in Egypt isn't always straightforward. Between monthly bills, installment plans for appliances, electronics, or furniture, and the constant fluctuation of the dollar-to-pound exchange rate — keeping track of where your money goes can feel overwhelming.

El Mizan was built to fix that.

The idea started simple: a tool that speaks the language of the everyday Egyptian user. No complicated dashboards, no financial jargon — just a clean, intuitive way to log your expenses, track your installments, and always know where you stand.

One of El Mizan's standout features is its built-in currency converter. In a market where prices shift between dollars and Egyptian pounds constantly, El Mizan lets you enter amounts in either currency and instantly see the equivalent — so you're never caught off guard when budgeting for an imported product or an online purchase.

But the heart of El Mizan is its installment tracker. Whether you're paying off a phone, a fridge, or a loan, El Mizan keeps a clear record of what you owe, how many payments are left, and when the next one is due — all in one place.

Built with simplicity as a core value, El Mizan is designed so that anyone — regardless of their tech experience — can open it and immediately feel in control of their finances.

Because everyone deserves to find their balance.
